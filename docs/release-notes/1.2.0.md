**Flip and Burn 1.2.0**: moon trips, and trips that fly on without you.

### What's new

- **Moon trips.** Fly from a planet's orbit to one of its moons, from a moon back to its planet, or from one moon to
  another (the Mun to Minmus). See [moon trips](https://github.com/kbennett2000/flip-and-burn/blob/main/docs/ways-to-fly.md#moon-trips).
  - **Fly to** lists the planet's moons first, then the planets.
  - When the planet, or the moon you're leaving, is in the way, the ship waits in its orbit, engine off and on time
    warp, until it has come round. Waiting costs no fuel, and the trip time includes it.
  - The plan allows for the planet's gravity along the way, and goes around the planet when it sits between two moons.
  - If an acceleration is too low to leave a low orbit or to stop at the target, the planner says so and names the
    least that works.
- **Fleet flights: leave a trip and it flies on without you.** Switch to another ship, go to the Space Center or the
  Tracking Station: the trip keeps flying, with the same steering and safety net, at any time warp. See
  [fleet flights](https://github.com/kbennett2000/flip-and-burn/blob/main/docs/saving-and-pausing.md#fleet-flights-trips-that-fly-without-you).
  - When it arrives, time warp drops to normal and a message names the ship, where it arrived and its orbit.
  - If warp is faster than Flip and Burn can fly the ship, warp drops to a rate it can keep up with, and a message says
    which ship is holding it, so the ship stays where it should be and arrives on time.
  - Fuel and electric charge are used as the burns use them. Several ships can fly at once.
- **Braking into strong gravity.** Diving into a deep well (Jool, Tylo, Laythe, Kerbin from its moons), the braking is
  planned a little below your acceleration, so there's push to spare at the end. The trip window says so while it
  brakes.
- **Better fuel figures.**
  - Slowing down into a strong pull costs more than the speed you arrive with; the planner prices it from that and picks
    a slower trip when a fast one would eat into your 10% reserve.
  - KSP Interstellar's fusion drives use more fuel at part throttle; the plan and the trip window count it.
  - The reserve the window shows during a coast trip matches what you arrive with to within about a point once the
    braking begins, and in testing all the way (trips to Duna and Tylo). On some coasts into strong gravity it can be
    up to about 2 points off before the braking starts.

### Fixes since 1.1.0

- Leaving a planet with the target on its far side no longer wastes fuel burning away from it.
- The last burn holds the parking height on a fast arrival, and every trip ends with a gentle trim, so slow arrivals at
  small planets don't end in a lopsided orbit.

### Not yet

- Sending a ship on a trip from the Tracking Station without flying it first (a later release).
- A moon straight to another planet's system: get into orbit around the moon's planet first.

### Requirements

- Kerbal Space Program **1.12.x**
- **MechJeb2 2.15 or later**: https://github.com/MuMech/MechJeb2/releases
- **Module Manager** (MechJeb needs it): https://github.com/sarbian/ModuleManager/releases

### Installing or updating

1. Download **FlipAndBurn-1.2.0.zip** below.
2. Copy the `FlipAndBurn` folder from inside the zip's `GameData` folder into your own `GameData` folder, replacing the old one.
3. In orbit, open MechJeb's menu and click **Flip and Burn**.

Trips saved with 1.1.0 carry on in 1.2.0.
